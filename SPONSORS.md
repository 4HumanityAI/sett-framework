# Sponsoring SETT

SETT is an independent, solo project. Sponsorship doesn't change what's
free: everything in this repository is MIT-licensed and stays that way,
for anyone, forever. Sponsoring is a way to say the project is useful to
you and to help it get more attention and maintenance time.

You can sponsor through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/EduDanielV).

## Tiers

Each tier includes everything from the tiers below it.

### $1 / month - Thanks
- Our gratitude, plain and simple. Every bit helps keep the project
  going, no tier required to mean that.

### $3 / month - Supporter
- Everything above.
- A Sponsor badge next to your name on GitHub.

### $5 / month - Backer
- Everything above.
- Your name listed in the [Sponsors](#current-sponsors) section below.

### $10 / month - Contributor
- Everything above.
- Your name listed in the main [`README.md`](README.md).

### $20 / month - Insider
- Everything above.
- Early notes on what's planned for the next release, ahead of the
  public CHANGELOG entry.

### $30 / month - Priority
- Everything above.
- Priority triage: issues you open get looked at first. This does not
  mean your issue gets fixed first, or ahead of a correctness or safety
  problem; it means it gets read and answered first.

### $50 / month - Advisor
- Everything above.
- An invitation to weigh in on SETT's direction: design discussions for
  upcoming features, before they're settled.

### $100 / month - Champion
- Everything above.
- Your name or logo in the sponsors section on
  [4humanityAI.com](https://4humanityai.com).

One-time sponsorships are also welcome and are thanked the same way a
single month at that tier would be.

## A note on public listing

Sponsorship is never refused. What's at my discretion is the *public
display* of a name, handle, or logo, whether in this document, the README, or
on [4humanityAI.com](https://4humanityai.com). Nothing offensive, no
hate symbols, no unrelated business plugs, no lookalike or scam
branding. If something submitted for display doesn't fit, the
sponsorship stays and I'll reach out about the listing; it's not a
reason to decline the sponsorship itself.

## What sponsorship does not buy

No tier grants influence over the [Ethics Ruler](docs/concepts.md) or any
safety-relevant decision. No tier grants early or special access to
security fixes. No tier changes the license or creates a paid version of
anything in this repository. These are deliberate limits, not
oversights.

## Why sponsor a framework instead of an app

SETT itself has no end users: it is what a real application gets built
on top of. If you're using SETT, or a project you depend on is,
sponsoring is a way to fund the framework layer directly rather than
only the applications built on it.
